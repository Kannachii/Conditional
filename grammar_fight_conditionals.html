GRAMMAR REFERENCE

🟢 ZERO CONDITIONAL

Use for: Facts, General Truths, Things always true

Structure: If + present simple ➔ present simple

Example: "If you exercise, you feel healthier."

🟡 FIRST CONDITIONAL

Use for: Real Future Possibilities

Structure: If + present simple ➔ will + verb

Example: "If it rains, we will stay indoors."

🔴 SECOND CONDITIONAL

Use for: Imaginary Situations / Giving Advice

Structure: If + past simple ➔ would + verb

Example: "If I were you, I would join the aerobics class."

HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. 2 Players on 1 Screen: Players take turns answering English conditional questions.
  • 2. Correct Answers Attack: Answering correctly attacks the opponent, builds combos, and charges your ULTIMATE METER!
  • 3. Wrong Answers / Timeout: Missing an answer causes a failed strike and resets your combo streak.
  • 4. Ultimate Attack: Fill your meter to 100% (5 correct answers) to launch a devastating 25 HP attack on your next correct turn!
